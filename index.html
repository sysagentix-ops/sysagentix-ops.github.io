NEXUS.

About

NEXUS is a research project on the automated generation of structured system models from technical documentation.

Overview

Unstructured technical PDFs are a central bottleneck in the adoption of model-based systems engineering. NEXUS converts them into valid SysML v2 models through a multi-stage, human-supervised process that keeps domain experts in control at every step.

Research context

NEXUS is an independent research project. It was published at IECON 2026, the 52nd Annual Conference of the IEEE Industrial Electronics Society in Doha, Qatar, and at the AgenticUQ Workshop at ICML 2026. The project continues this work on connecting technical documentation to formal, model-based systems engineering.
